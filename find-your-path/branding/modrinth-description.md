# Find Your Path

**A friendly in-game guide for brand-new Minecraft players.** No wiki, no YouTube: just press **G**.

Minecraft has no single goal, so new players often don't know what to do. Find Your Path teaches the basics one small step at a time, then helps you discover what kind of player you are.

## How it works

**Level 1: Survive Your First Day.** A guided quest through the essentials: wood, planks, a crafting table, tools, a stone sword, torches, food, and a safe shelter for your first night. Each step pops up on a glowing panel when the last one is done.

**Level 2: Find your path.** Finishing your first day unlocks five paths:
- **Builder**: make places, from a stone house up.
- **Explorer**: build a boat, travel far, climb high.
- **Adventurer**: upgrade to iron and win a fight.
- **Engineer**: build your first machine.
- **Homesteader**: plant a farm and build a pen.

Paths build on Level 1. Some steps ask you to remember recipes, with a "Show me how" button if you're stuck.

## Features
- **Live crafting coach.** In your inventory or crafting table, the squares to fill glow, wrong items turn red, and a panel tells you the next move.
- **Side lessons while you wait for night.** Make a chest, cook food, or make a bed, each labeled with how long it takes and how long until dark.
- **Tips.** 12 essential tips pop up as you play, and you can browse them anytime from the menu.
- **Gentle help when things go wrong.** The first time you die, it explains what happened to your stuff.

## Requirements
- Minecraft Java **26.2**
- **Fabric Loader** and **Fabric API**
- Client-side only. The Training Zombie in the Adventurer path needs single-player or a LAN world you host.
